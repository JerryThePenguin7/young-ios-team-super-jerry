# 10 — Test with a real older user (and get impact numbers)

"Real-world impact" weighs the most in judging. The strongest proof is a real grandparent using Jenny, plus numbers.
Use **made-up envelopes only** (the 5 fake envelopes in [docs/pill-reader/plan.md](../../docs/pill-reader/plan.md) section 10.1), never their real medicines or health data (PDPA).

## 1. Make the test plan

```text
Read README.md (The idea), DESIGN.md (who we design for), and docs/design/research.md.
Write a 15-minute usability test for a Thai adult aged 60+ using Jenny on the iPad, in simple Thai,
with these tasks:
1. Take a photo of a fake medicine envelope and say what the medicine is and how many to take.
2. Save it.
3. Tick this morning's dose.
4. Find out what time the next pill is.
For each task, list: what to say (without hints), what counts as success, and what to measure
(time in seconds, number of mistakes, whether they needed help). Add a before/after comparison:
the same questions answered from the paper envelope alone, then with Jenny.
End with 3 short questions to ask afterwards (what was hard, would they use it, would their family).
Keep it kind: they are testing the app, not being tested.
```

## 2. During the test (by hand)
- [ ] Ask permission first. Record only times and mistakes, not their name or their health information
- [ ] Don't help unless they're stuck for more than 30 seconds (write down "needed help")
- [ ] Use a timer for each task, envelope alone vs Jenny
- [ ] Write down their exact words when they get confused: these are your best design fixes

## 3. Turn it into pitch numbers

```text
Here are our test results: [paste the table]. Calculate:
- Average time to answer "what and how many" with the envelope alone vs with Jenny
- Mistakes with the envelope vs with Jenny
- How many people finished each task without help
Write 2–3 honest sentences for the pitch (say how many people we tested, don't overclaim),
and the 3 design problems we saw most, with a fix for each.
```

Put the numbers in the README "Impact" line and in the pitch ([07-pitch-prep.md](07-pitch-prep.md)).
