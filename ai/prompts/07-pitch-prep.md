# 07 — Pitch prep

The pitch is **5 minutes + ~2 minutes of judge Q&A**. Everyone on the team must be able to answer questions (that's "Ownership" in the judging).

```text
Read ai/what-we-are-building.md (judging criteria) and README.md (The idea).
Help us write a 5-minute pitch for judges from different industries. Structure:
1. The person and their problem (a real story, 30s)
2. Why it matters (numbers: time, money, mistakes) (30s)
3. Live demo of the one core feature (2 min)
4. Why we chose [our AI option], and why it runs on-device / why the cluster (45s)
5. Impact + what's next (45s)
Then write 10 tough questions judges might ask (on impact, AI choice, privacy, accuracy, what happens
offline), and a short answer for each. Assign each question to a teammate: [names].
Slides will be a PDF with no animations and no unauthorized logos.
```

Practice out loud with a timer. Each teammate should present at least one part.
