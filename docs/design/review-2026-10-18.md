## Design review: main's home screen and log (2026-10-18)

Run with the `jenny-design-review` skill on `main` (commit `bf80c30`, PR #2) after merging it into `feature/pill-reader`.
Evidence: iPhone 17e and iPad Pro 11" simulators. Light and dark mode, Thai and English, default and the largest accessibility text size. All medicines are made up.

### Scores before the fixes

| Area | Score | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Accessibility | 2/4 | VoiceOver labels and hints are good, but the week strip, red box, Logged rows and reminders card squeezed or clipped at the biggest text size |
| Older users | 3/4 | Big rows, read aloud, Skipped / Taken. But a bare "+" to log, and a hard-to-read Late badge |
| Apple platform fit | 4/4 | Very close to Health's Medications page: week strip, Log, Logged, the log sheet, NavigationStack, SF Symbols |
| Follows DESIGN.md | 3/4 | Consistent tokens and components. The `.caption` white-on-orange badge broke the contrast rule |
| Adapts to device | 3/4 | Good two-column iPad layout, but the red box text was cut off ("และบัน…", "เลือกจากคลัง…") in the iPad column |
| Clarity & trust | 3/4 | Clear empty states, disclaimer, "photos stay on this device". A syringe icon for diabetes tablets could scare or mislead |
| **Total** | **18/24** | |

### What's working (keep these)
- It feels like Apple Health: a fluent iPhone user trusts it in the first 10 seconds.
- The log sheet has "Log All as Taken", per-medicine **Skipped / Taken**, read aloud, and **Done** pinned at the bottom.
- Thai and English work fully and separately, including Thai times ("8:00 น.") and the Thai weekday letters.
- Medicines are grouped by what they're for, with a read-aloud button on every card.

### Fixed in this branch
1. **P1 · `Views/LogTimeCard.swift`:** the bare "+" became a labeled **"+ บันทึก / Log"** pill (44 pt tall, light red so it doesn't compete with the red box). Who it hurts: the grandmother, who doesn't know what "+" means.
2. **P1 · `Views/LogTimeCard.swift`:** the Late and Time-to-take badges were white `.caption` text on orange. They're now dark `.subheadline.bold` text on a light tint with a colored icon. Who it hurts: everyone with weaker eyes.
3. **P1 · `Views/WeekStrip.swift`:** the day letters were clipped ("M", "W") at the biggest text sizes. Their size is now capped at xxxLarge, and press-and-hold shows the full day (the Large Content Viewer).
4. **P1 · `Views/CameraCard.swift`:**
   - At the biggest text sizes, the icon now goes above the title.
   - On iPad the subtitle wraps instead of ending in "…".
   - The two buttons always stack, so "เลือกจากคลังภาพ" is never cut off.
5. **P2 · `Views/LoggedCard.swift` and `ContentView.swift`:** with big text, the "8:00 AM / 3 hr. ago" rows and the reminders card stack instead of squeezing.
6. **P2 · `Models/MedicineGroup.swift`:** the diabetes group icon changed from a syringe to a drop (like Health's blood glucose), so tablets don't look like injections.

All builds pass, and each fix was checked in the simulator.

### Still open
- **P2:** the toolbar's Add and Settings are icon-only. VoiceOver labels exist, and the system shows the Large Content Viewer for bar buttons. But a labeled "เพิ่มยา" button somewhere on the home screen would help older users (Liu 2021).
- **P2:** the week strip's future days are empty gray circles. They could be lighter, or hidden, so they don't look like missed days.
- **P3:** idea #1 in `research.md`: when a pill reminder is ticked in Apple Reminders, log the dose in Jenny too.
